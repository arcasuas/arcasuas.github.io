03 / Our mission

BUILT AT THE INTERSECTION OF ENERGY & FLIGHT

The flight changes.
The estimate
should too.

PURDUE ROOTSOPERATOR FIRSTIN DEVELOPMENT

Can we finish the flight—and still get home?

Arcas UAS began with the energy-awareness challenge explored through Purdue’s Hacking for Defense program. UAS operators need to understand remaining endurance as flight profiles and conditions change.

A simple charge icon leaves too much interpretation to the person flying. Our hypothesis is that combining battery properties with wind, weather, flight path, and prior use can produce a more useful estimate of remaining energy and distance.

We’re developing a Smart BMS concept that translates that context into a simple operator display, with a protected reserve and a clear prompt to return.
